How the GroundingtoGo Quiz can help you find the type of solo trip you actually need right now

09.08.20266 min read

You’ve felt it, haven’t you? That quiet pull to just go somewhere on your own. To swap the routine for a new horizon and come back feeling a little more like yourself. And then you open a browser tab, type “where should I travel,” and drown in two hundred listicles that all say the same thing — and somehow you’re further from a decision than when you started.

That’s exactly the moment the GroundingtoGo solo travel quiz was made for.

Why “where should I go?” is the wrong first question

Most solo trip planning starts with a place. A destination someone raved about, a photo that lived in your head, a flight deal that felt like a sign. But when you plan a trip around a place before you understand what you actually need from it, you can end up somewhere beautiful and still feel restless — because the trip didn’t match the person taking it.

The better first question isn’t where — it’s why now. Are you burnt out and craving stillness? Restless and hungry for novelty? Quietly lonely and wanting easy connection? Each of those points to a completely different kind of solo trip, even if the map looks the same.

The GroundingtoGo quiz starts there: with you, not the destination.

What the solo travel quiz actually measures

This isn’t a “which European city are you?” personality quiz. It’s built on a simple idea — that a good solo trip should fit three things at once:

  • Your personality — how you like to move through a place, whether you recharge alone or around others, how much structure you want versus room to wander.
  • Your wellbeing right now — the honest state you’re in this season, not the highlight-reel version. Tired, anxious, flat, or simply ready for something new.
  • What you’re actually looking for — rest, adventure, meaning, connection, or a proper reset.

Twelve short questions read those signals and translate them into a trip that suits this version of you — the one reading this today.

How the GroundingtoGo quiz works

It’s designed to be the opposite of overwhelming:

  • Twelve questions, no wrong answers. They’re about how you feel and how you like to travel, not trivia.
  • Nothing to sign up for. You get your full result without leaving a single detail. (You can choose to, if you’d like more — but you never have to.)
  • Two destinations, not one. At the end you get a place close to home and one further afield, so you can pick between an easy escape and a bigger adventure.
  • A suggested way to spend your time that matches your energy — because a solo trip is as much about how you travel as where.

Every result is a real, considered place — somewhere genuinely worth going as a woman travelling on her own.

Why this matters for solo female travel

Planning solo female travel carries a layer most quizzes ignore: safety, comfort, and the quiet confidence of knowing a place actually works for a woman on her own. Every destination the quiz can suggest has been chosen with that in mind — not just “is it beautiful,” but “would I send a friend here alone, and feel good about it?”

That’s the difference between a generic travel personality quiz and one built specifically to help women take solo trips that feel as safe as they do freeing.

Who it’s for

You’ll get the most from the quiz if any of this sounds familiar:

  • You want to travel solo but the blank map feels overwhelming — and part of you isn’t sure it’s safe to figure out alone.
  • You don’t want just another holiday. You want time that genuinely resets you.
  • You’ve travelled plenty, and you’re simply tired of spending your evenings second-guessing whether you’ve planned the right trip.

Whichever one is you, the quiz meets you there.

What you do with your result

Here’s the honest part: your result is a starting point, not the whole trip. It tells you the kind of place and pace that fit you right now — the hardest decision to make alone, finally made simpler. From there, you can plan it yourself with a clearer sense of direction, or hand the details to me and get a solo itinerary built entirely around your answers.

Either way, you leave with something most people never get before they book: a trip that fits who you are, not just where the deals are.

Ready to find your kind of trip?

If you’ve been circling the idea of a solo trip without landing on one, this is the gentlest possible first step. No sign-up, no pressure, no wrong answers — just twelve questions and a place that finally feels like yours.
